Ghana’s Hidden Gems

The places most visitors never reach — waterfalls, stilt villages, ancient parks, and mountain trails

Northern Region

Mole National Park

Ghana’s largest wildlife park. Elephants, antelopes, and walking safaris in the wild.

~8–10 hrs from Accra

Volta Region

Wli Waterfalls

Highest waterfall in West Africa. Lower and upper falls, forest trails, and cool spray.

~4–5 hrs from Accra

Central Region

Kakum National Park

Famous canopy walkway high above the rainforest. Birds, monkeys, and dense jungle.

~3 hrs from Accra

Western Region

Nzulezo Stilt Village

Ancient village built entirely on stilts over a lake. Boat access only. Living history.

~5–6 hrs from Accra

Volta Region

Mount Afadjato

Ghana’s highest peak. Steep hike through forest with panoramic views at the summit.

~4–5 hrs from Accra

Volta Region

Tafi Atome Monkey Sanctuary

Sacred forest where monkeys live freely among the village. Quiet and deeply local.

~4 hrs from Accra

Northern Region

Larabanga Mosque

One of the oldest mosques in West Africa (15th century). Sudanese-style mud architecture near Mole.

Near Mole National Park

Upper East Region

Paga Crocodile Pond

Sacred pond where crocodiles are friendly and revered. Unique cultural experience near the Burkina border.

~2 hrs from Bolgatanga
